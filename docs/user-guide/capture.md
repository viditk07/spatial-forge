# Photograph Capture Guide

Use the phone's main rear camera, keep one focal length, and disable digital zoom. Walk a
steady path around the room or structure with about 70–80% overlap between photographs.
For a normal room, 100–250 sharp images is a useful starting range.

- Capture the full perimeter, corners, wall-floor boundaries, and wall-ceiling boundaries.
- Add temporary removable markers to blank walls when permitted.
- Keep lighting consistent and avoid harsh reflections, mirrors, and transparent surfaces.
- Do not move furniture during capture; keep people and pets out of the scene.
- Hold the phone steadily and retake blurred frames.
- Avoid switching between ultrawide, main, and telephoto lenses.
- Transfer original files to the laptop without social-media or messaging compression.

More images are not automatically better. Repeated frames, motion, featureless walls, and
mixed lenses can reduce registration quality. Review SpatialForge's warnings before running
the expensive pipeline; feature-density scores are estimates, not guarantees.
